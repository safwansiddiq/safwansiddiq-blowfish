## The Story: Systems Thinker. Creative at Heart.

My journey into tech started with an Electrical & Electronics Engineering degree — which gave me a deep, almost instinctive understanding of how systems work. But I quickly realized that engineering alone didn't let me fully be myself. I wanted to build things that communicated, inspired, and connected with people.

So I made an unusual move: I stepped into content production at a social enterprise creating learning materials for Malaysian schoolkids — think Khan Academy, but homegrown. That's where I found my sweet spot: applying systems thinking to creative work. The precision of a well-designed production pipeline. The craft of storytelling that actually lands with an audience.

That combination has followed me everywhere since — through roles in digital marketing, design, enterprise architecture consulting, and Learning & Development at a global manufacturing software company. Different industries, different titles — but the same underlying obsession: how do you build something that works beautifully and means something?

## The Thread: Always at the Frontier.

![Business Growth and Efficiency Chart](/img/about/growth_chart.png)

There's a pattern people who know me tend to notice. I show up to things early — not because I'm chasing hype, but because I genuinely find new things interesting before they become obvious to everyone else.

### Podcasting — 2014

I co-founded a podcast studio and produced several shows — a current affairs show, a spoken word poetry show, a creatives-by-creatives show — back when most people in Malaysia had never listened to a podcast, let alone made one. I was told more than once that I got in too early. Maybe. But the curiosity was real, and so was the craft.

### No-Code Development — 2020

During the pandemic, I taught myself no-code app development. The result? A prototype I built for a foundation I was working at beat three professional software houses in a formal evaluation — and would have saved tens of thousands in development costs. People told me they had never even heard of no-code as a concept until I showed them what it could do.

### AI Automation — Now

When the AI wave arrived, I was already building. I've since developed a growing suite of automation projects — an intelligent email sorter, a newsletter aggregator and summarizer, a YouTube note-taker, and more. Each one a real proof of concept for what becomes possible when you wire AI thoughtfully into everyday workflows.

> "I don't chase trends. I just tend to find interesting things interesting before they become obvious to everyone else — and I can help bring your business there too."
